## About Me

I build practical AI systems that translate complex knowledge into useful products and workflows.

My work sits at the intersection of agent systems, software engineering, automation, and real-world operations. I’m especially interested in how models use tools, retain context, coordinate work, and become more useful over time.

I’m especially interested in:

- AI product engineering
- Agent architecture and orchestration
- Memory, retrieval, and context systems
- Developer tooling and automation
- Quantitative and decision-support systems
- Turning operational expertise into software

## Focus Areas

- Building AI systems designed for real work, not just demonstrations
- Connecting models with tools, data, memory, and human expertise
- Designing reliable workflows around agents and automation
- Exploring efficient model architectures and open AI research
- Turning ambiguous ideas into working systems with clear iteration loops
